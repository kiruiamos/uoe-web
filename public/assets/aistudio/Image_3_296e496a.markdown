## Brand & Style

This design system captures the vibrant spirit of Chepkoilel—fusing academic rigor with the warmth of highland community life. The aesthetic reflects Eldoret’s evergreen topography, agricultural heritage, and lively student pulse.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Grounded & Academic:** Instills trust and reliability for essential tasks such as fee clearance, exam timetables, and hostel allocation.
- **Vibrant & Communal:** Radiates peer energy, social belonging, and campus pride across clubs, mess hall menus, and sports events.
- **Approachable & Uncluttered:** Eliminates institutional bureaucracy through legible typographic scales, high contrast touchpoints, and clear, welcoming microcopy.

### Design Direction: Modern Organic Neo-Campus
The visual language blends structured modern UI utility with soft, organic tactile elements. Interfaces employ layered warm ivory surfaces, forest-tinted boundaries, and lively sun-soaked accents to maintain clarity while remaining distinctly local, optimistic, and student-focused.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional gravitas with youthful campus energy, anchored by Eldoret's lush highland landscape and equatorial sunlight.

### Color Roles & Hierarchy
- **Deep Highland Green (`#0B5345`):** The primary anchor. Used for primary navigation headers, principal call-to-action buttons, verified institutional badges, and prominent brand accents.
- **Fresh Emerald (`#27AE60`):** The secondary functional color. Represents active states, success notices, mess opening hours, available hostel slots, and live academic statuses.
- **Golden Sun Accent (`#F39C12`):** The energetic tertiary punch. Reserved for urgent announcements, pending fee/course deadlines, event highlights, student welfare alerts, and rating badges.
- **Dark Slate (`#1E293B`):** The primary neutral for body text and deep contrast elements, providing superior readability over off-white backgrounds without the harsh glare of pure black.
- **Warm Ivory (`#F4F6F4` / `#F8F9FA`):** Ambient background surfaces and sub-layer containers, delivering an organic paper-like canvas that softens mobile screen fatigue.
- **Functional Warning & Error:** Derived using warm crimson (`#D9381E`) for overdue notices and warnings, balanced with muted olive-tinted dividers (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly to establish a friendly, contemporary, and highly legible experience across dense tables, timetable matrices, and quick-scan announcements.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Headlines:** Set in Bold (`700`) and ExtraBold (`800`) weights with slight negative letter tracking to give section headers presence, warmth, and structural authority.
- **Body:** Standardized on Regular (`400`) and Medium (`500`) weights with generous line-height to guarantee high legibility for outdoor smartphone usage under equatorial midday sunlight.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered in SemiBold (`600`) and Bold (`700`) with modest letter spacing for crisp badge readability, portal status pills, timetable room identifiers, and navigation tabs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a flexible 4-column structure for mobile devices and an 8-column layout for tablets, conforming to a strict 8pt base grid rhythm with a secondary 4pt baseline for inline compact badges.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Screen Margins:** Fixed at `1rem` (`16px`) on mobile viewports to preserve maximum data density for grade checks and mess queues, scaling up to `1.5rem` (`24px`) on tablets.
- **Card-Internal Density:** Balanced via `space-md` (`1rem`) to keep hostel listings and course blocks cohesive while preventing touch errors.
- **Stack Gaps:** Sequential section stacking enforces a `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) vertical rhythm to clearly distinguish disparate campus domains (e.g., student balance, today’s lectures, mess menu).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy drop shadows, opting instead for a layered highland topography of warm ivory tint surfaces, subtle dark slate outlines, and lush ambient glow.

### Depth Layers
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `background: #F4F6F4`. A warm, muted ivory ground tone.
- **Card Tier (Level 1):** `background: #FFFFFF`, complemented by a crisp hairline border (`border: 1px solid rgba(11, 83, 69, 0.08)`) and an ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 83, 69, 0.06)`).
- **Interactive Floating Tier (Level 2):** Quick action shortcuts, bottom sheets, and sticky fee paybars utilize an elevated diffused shadow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 83, 69, 0.12)`).
- **Modals & Overlays (Level 3):** Frosted ambient backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, `background: rgba(14, 41, 34, 0.4)`), drawing user focus to student clearance validations or urgent welfare hotlines.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a welcoming **Rounded** approach (`0.5rem` / `8px` base curve), conveying friendliness and accessibility.

### Shape Tiers
- **Input Fields & Small Cards (`rounded` - 8px):** Applied to form elements, table rows, timetable slots, and notification alerts.
- **Standard Cards & Modules (`rounded-lg` - 16px):** Used across academic feed cards, hostel accommodation summaries, and mess meal counters.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers (`rounded-xl` - 24px top corners):** Softens major transitions, anchoring popups naturally into the thumb reach zone.
- **Badges, Pills & Action Floating Buttons (Pill - 9999px):** Status chips, live cafeteria meal tags, fee clearance tags, and student club filters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#0B5345` with crisp white text, `height: 48px`, border radius `12px`. Subtle press animation (`scale: 0.98`).
- **Secondary:** Surface `#E8F5E9` with `#0B5345` label, zero border, optimal for secondary portal workflows.
- **Accent Action:** Filled `#F39C12` with dark slate `#1E293B` text for urgent welfare contacts, bursary requests, and cart/token actions.

### Cards
- Standard student cards (Mess, Hostel, Timetable) utilize white backgrounds on the Ivory canvas with `border-radius: 16px`, `padding: 16px`, and an outer border of `rgba(11, 83, 69, 0.08)`. Headers within cards often pair an energetic mini-icon container (colored `#F4F6F4` with an emerald icon).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Fully rounded (`9999px`), `font-size: 12px`, `font-weight: 600`.
  - *Open / Cleared:* Pale emerald background (`#E8F8F0`), `#1E7E46` text.
  - *Pending / Due:* Pale gold background (`#FEF5E7`), `#B7791F` text.
  - *Emergency / Closed:* Soft coral background (`#FDECE8`), `#C53030` text.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Campus Service Links:** Divided list rows with leading 40px rounded squircle icons (colored in matching highland forest tints) and chevron indicators.
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Solid `#FFFFFF` container with top border `1px solid rgba(11, 83, 69, 0.06)`. Selected items highlight with `#0B5345` and an active indicator dot in `#27AE60`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** `height: 48px`, border radius `8px`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus state features an outline ring in Fresh Emerald (`#27AE60`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Colored with Deep Highland Green (`#0B5345`), rounded with smooth transitions for course selections and student union polling ballots.

### Specialized Campus Modules
- **Mess Counter Card:** Dynamic badge showing realtime cafeteria occupancy, live meal period (e.g., "Lunch: 11:30 AM - 2:30 PM"), and affordable student menu items.
- **Student ID Digital Pass:** Deep Highland Green gradient card with embossed university watermark patterns, gold student status seal, high-contrast barcode/QR code, and active term registration pill.
